## Best-supported cause
The strongest explanation is **provider authorization degradation, amplified by v2.14.0’s more patient payment-client profile**. The provider’s reported impact overlaps the checkout slowdown, and payment logs show requests hitting the new 8-second timeout and retrying. The release also permits more attempts and longer backoff, so stalled authorizations can keep checkout requests waiting longer than under v17. [provider-status:L5-L9] [payment-client-log:L187-L208] [config-diff:L30-L47] This supports an interaction, but the available evidence does not quantify how much latency each factor contributed.

## Competing explanation
The main competitor is DB load from the concurrent reindex. Its timing overlaps the onset, so it could have contributed. However, during the high-CPU period, query p95 was 24–30ms and pool-wait p95 was 1–3ms; checkout remained slow after DB CPU returned to normal, while provider impact continued. That makes DB load a weaker explanation for the multi-second checkout tail than the authorization path. [db-change-log:L5-L8] [db-metrics:L132-L163] [checkout-metrics:L141-L186] [provider-status:L5-L9]

## Recommended mitigation
Evaluate a **payment-client profile-only rollback from v18 to v17**, rather than rolling back the entire release. The runbook describes this config rollback as reversible and taking about 60 seconds, with incident-commander approval required. Preserve retries with the same idempotency key and do not disable fraud screening or weaken payment safety checks. [runbook-payments:L3-L8] [runbook-payments:L10-L15] If approved and performed, follow the runbook’s 15-minute latency/error-ratio watch and reconciliation check for duplicate charges. [runbook-payments:L19-L22] No production change has been made as part of this read-only investigation.

## Uncertainty
The evidence supports the provider-plus-client-profile explanation but does not establish a precise causal split. The logs inspected are selected samples, and sampled address-validation spans were fast and successful but do not establish the full request path. [traces-2026-10-06:L207-L495] Further confidence would require full-incident payment timeout/retry counts and complete slow-trace span trees correlated with checkout duration. Before any mitigation, confirm the profile rollback’s exact resulting settings and expected customer impact with the incident commander. [runbook-payments:L10-L15]
